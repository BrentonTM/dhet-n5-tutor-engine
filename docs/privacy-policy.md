# Trentox Tutor AI Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Trentox Tutor AI handles information in its web app. Contact the Trentox team at [trentoxtx@gmail.com](mailto:trentoxtx@gmail.com) for privacy questions and requests.

## Information we use

- **Sign-in and profile.** Google and Streamlit handle Google sign-in. The sign-in integration receives verified Google account information, which can include your account identifier, name and email address. Trentox checks the sign-in and derives an internal learner identifier to keep study sessions separate. You choose a nickname for Tex. Trentox does not receive your Google password.
- **Study activity.** Trentox uses your selected course, answers, scores, confidence ratings, progress, lab reflections and reminder preferences to provide the study workspace. Importing a progress file sends that file to the app for processing. Downloaded progress reports and calendar files are saved wherever you choose on your device.
- **AI requests.** When you ask Tex for help, generate a lesson or quiz, submit work for review, or request transcription, Trentox sends the information needed for that feature to Google Gemini. This may include your nickname, recent chat context, study context, typed work, recordings, transcripts, or a submitted document, image or text extracted from it. Remove names, student numbers and other private information from work you send for AI feedback.
- **Technical information.** Streamlit Community Cloud hosts the app and may process connection information, cookies, usage information and operational logs to provide and protect the service. Your browser may connect to Cloudflare's cdnjs or jsDelivr to load the software used to display 3D models. Those services receive standard connection information such as your IP address.

## How information is processed

Trentox uses this information for sign-in, tutoring, transcription and translation, study tools and troubleshooting. The hosted app uses HTTPS. Google and Streamlit process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Gemini's handling of requests and responses depends on the service configuration and applicable terms, including its rules for logging, safety review and product improvement. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) · [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) · [Streamlit Privacy Notice](https://streamlit.io/privacy-policy)

## Retention and choices

Trentox keeps study activity in the active server session. It does not provide a permanent cloud study profile or sync study history across devices. You can clear the current Tex conversation from the chat and sign out to clear the active study session and remove the Trentox sign-in cookie from that browser. Other open sessions and copies you downloaded can remain. Google and Streamlit apply their own retention policies to the data they process. Clearing chat or signing out cannot recall requests already processed by those services.

## Privacy and deletion requests

To ask for access, correction or deletion of information held by Trentox, email [trentoxtx@gmail.com](mailto:trentoxtx@gmail.com?subject=Trentox%20privacy%20request). Include a brief description of what you want us to review. We may ask for enough information to verify the request. Never send passwords or copies of student work just to identify your request.

You can make a request without signing in. To manage your Google account itself, use [Google Account settings](https://myaccount.google.com/).
