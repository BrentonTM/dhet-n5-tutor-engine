# Trentox Tutor AI Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains how Trentox Tutor AI handles information in its web app. Contact the Trentox team at [trentoxtx@gmail.com](mailto:trentoxtx@gmail.com) for privacy questions and requests.

## Information we use

- **Sign-in and profile.** Streamlit handles sign-in through configured providers: Google, and Microsoft or Apple through a compatible broker when enabled. The sign-in integration receives verified provider account information, which can include your account identifier, name and email address. Trentox checks the sign-in and derives an internal learner identifier to keep study sessions separate. You choose a nickname for Tex. Trentox does not receive your provider password. Accounts from different providers are not automatically linked by email.
- **Study activity.** Trentox uses your selected course, answers, scores, confidence ratings, progress, lab reflections and reminder preferences to provide the study workspace. Importing a progress file sends that file to the app for processing. Downloaded progress reports and calendar files are saved wherever you choose on your device.
- **AI requests.** When you ask Tex for help, generate a lesson or quiz, submit work for review, or request transcription, Trentox sends the information needed for that feature to Google Gemini. This may include your nickname, recent chat context, study context, typed work, recordings, transcripts, or a submitted document, image or text extracted from it. Remove names, student numbers and other private information from work you send for AI feedback.
- **Optional voice.** Opening the voice panel loads Puter.js from js.puter.com, which receives standard connection information. Enabling voice signs you in to Puter. When you request speech or opt into automatic playback, Tex's spoken reply is sent through Puter to ElevenLabs. Puter account credits and limits apply. Live microphone transcription uses your browser's speech-recognition service, then sends the transcript to Gemini. The recorded-answer input sends recordings to Gemini instead. You may type without enabling either microphone.
- **Technical information.** Streamlit Community Cloud hosts the app and may process connection information, cookies, usage information and operational logs to provide and protect the service. Your browser may connect to Cloudflare's cdnjs or jsDelivr to load the software used to display 3D models. Those services receive standard connection information such as your IP address.

## How information is processed

Trentox uses this information for sign-in, tutoring, transcription and translation, study tools and troubleshooting. The hosted app uses HTTPS. Google and Streamlit process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Gemini's handling of requests and responses depends on the service configuration and applicable terms, including its rules for logging, safety review and product improvement. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) · [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) · [Streamlit Privacy Notice](https://streamlit.io/privacy-policy)

Optional voice processing also follows [Puter's privacy policy](https://puter.com/privacy) and [ElevenLabs' privacy policy](https://elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy). These services apply their own retention terms.

## Retention and choices

When account history is available, Trentox stores activity under an internal learner identifier in a server-side database. This includes dates, subjects, topics, topic-check scores, confidence ratings, missed-concept labels, whether a lab prediction matched, and records that you received practice feedback or submitted a mock exam. We use topic checks and lab reflections to identify concepts to review and suggest your next study activity. Saved history is available when you return with the same Google account. You can request its deletion using the contact below.

Your chats, recordings, uploaded work and written answers are excluded from the account history. Other workspace activity stays in the active server session. You can clear the current Tex conversation from the chat and sign out to clear the active study session and remove the Trentox sign-in cookie from that browser. Other open sessions and copies you downloaded can remain. Google and Streamlit apply their own retention policies to the data they process. Clearing chat or signing out cannot recall requests already processed by those services.

**Money Class and Career readiness.** When account history is available, Trentox saves module completion and completion dates for the Employability Meter. Practice budgets and CV drafts stay in your active session. If you choose Tex feedback on your CV, Trentox sends the draft and recent chat context to Google Gemini. Add private contact details to the downloaded draft on your device.

Interactive lesson conversations stay in the current study session and can be downloaded or ended and cleared. Stop voice turns off Tex's audio and live microphone. Puter sign-in is separate from Google/Trentox sign-in; use **Sign out of Puter** in the voice panel to remove that browser connection. Clearing a Trentox conversation does not delete provider records.

A first-use guide flag and first-shown time may be stored under your internal learner identifier to prevent repeat automatic tours. When account storage is unavailable, an account-specific browser cookie remembers this choice on that browser. You can replay the guide manually.

## Privacy and deletion requests

To ask for access, correction or deletion of information held by Trentox, email [trentoxtx@gmail.com](mailto:trentoxtx@gmail.com?subject=Trentox%20privacy%20request). Include a brief description of what you want us to review. We may ask for enough information to verify the request. Never send passwords or copies of student work just to identify your request.

You can make a request without signing in. To manage your Google account itself, use [Google Account settings](https://myaccount.google.com/).
