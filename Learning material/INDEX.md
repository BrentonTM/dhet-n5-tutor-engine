# Trentox Tutor AI learning-material index

This index catalogues the learner-provided files in this folder. The original filenames are retained so that documents remain easy to identify.

## Sorted layout

### Digital Electronics

- `Digital Electronics/N4/Assignments/Assignment 1 N4.pdf` — number-system arithmetic and conversions, BCD and other codes, and an AND-gate symbol, truth table, and Boolean expression.
- `Digital Electronics/Reference Library/REFERENCE ALIGNMENT MAP.md` — concise mapping from the book's chapters to the learner-confirmed N4 study groups and the provisional N5 outline. The app reads this note; it does not contain textbook passages.
- A user-supplied 741-page Digital Electronics textbook (Anil K. Maini, 2007) was reviewed locally to prepare the map. It is a broad reference, not an authenticated DHET syllabus. The full PDF stays in the local project and is not in the public app repository.
- Use the book only to explain aligned concepts, not to expand the confirmed course scope. K-maps remain included in N4; Memory Systems remain outside the learner-confirmed N4 scope.
- The N4 assignment is the other Digital Electronics source in the imported learning-material set. The project also has a working N4 topic map and reference screenshots, but these are not substitutes for an official syllabus.

### Industrial Electronics N5

- `Industrial Electronics/N5/00 Syllabus/N5 Industrial Electronics syllabus.pdf` — subject syllabus and module allocation.
- `Industrial Electronics/N5/01 AC and RLC/Module 01 Alternating current theory.pdf` — RC/RL integrators and differentiators, AC/RLC analysis, resonance, phasors, harmonics, and coupling.
- `Industrial Electronics/N5/01 AC and RLC/CHAPTER 01 TUT.pdf` — RLC and resonance calculation practice.
- `Industrial Electronics/N5/02 Power Supplies/Module 02 Power supplies.pdf` — waveform measures, rectification, filters, ripple, and regulation.
- `Industrial Electronics/N5/02 Power Supplies/CHAPTER 02 POWER SUPPLIES FULL LESSON.pptx` — 37-slide lesson on power supplies, filters, regulators, protection, and voltage multipliers.
- `Industrial Electronics/N5/02 Power Supplies/CHAPTER 02 TUT 01.pdf` — Chapter 2 practice sheet; text extraction is very limited, so check its pages before using it for AI tutoring.
- `Industrial Electronics/N5/03 Amplifiers/Module 03 Amplifiers.pdf` — transistor amplifier and operational-amplifier material. Its internal heading calls this Module 3 and covers both subjects.
- `Industrial Electronics/N5/03 Amplifiers/Chapter 03 transistor amplifiers.pptx` — 24-slide lesson on BJTs, fixed bias, stabilization, potential-divider bias, and h-equivalent circuits.
- `Industrial Electronics/N5/03 Amplifiers/Chapter 03 TRANSISTOR TUT 01.pdf` — transistor-amplifier practice; text extraction is very limited.
- `Industrial Electronics/N5/04 Integrated Circuits and Transducers/Module 04 integrated circuits and transducers.pdf` — ICs, ESD protection and handling, and transducer concepts.
- `Industrial Electronics/N5/04 Integrated Circuits and Transducers/integratd circuits.pdf` — CMOS handling and protection notes, labelled Chapter 05 in the document.
- The exact duplicate of the active Module 03 PDF is kept at repository path `Learning material Archive/Duplicates/Industrial Electronics N5/Module 03 Amplifiers (1).pdf`. It is preserved outside the tutor’s scan folder.

## Import-readiness notes

- The app walks subfolders under `Learning material`, so this subject-based filing keeps the materials discoverable.
- The Industrial Instruments textbook scan, the flow-measurement scan, and the pH-measurement scan have no selectable text. The app’s text-only material reader cannot learn from those pages yet; OCR or a text-based source is needed.
- The current app reader extracts only the first 15 pages and first 3,000 characters of each PDF. Full use of the 158-page Industrial Instruments scan needs OCR and page-aware ingestion; using the 741-page Digital Electronics book effectively also needs chapter-aware ingestion.
- The imported files do not provide an official Digital Electronics textbook or an authenticated detailed Digital Electronics N4/N5 syllabus. Do not treat the working topic maps as official syllabus documents.
